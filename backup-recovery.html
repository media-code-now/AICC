Services / Backup & Recovery

Know what is backed up, and how you would get it back.

Backups are only useful if they cover the right data, are protected from tampering, and can actually be restored. This service identifies your critical data, plans backup coverage and retention, monitors for failures, and tests recovery against priorities agreed with you.

Request a Security Assessment Book a Consultation

Business problems this service addresses

  • Uncertainty about which business data is backed up and which is not
  • Backups that fail silently without anyone noticing
  • Backups that could be deleted or changed by the same accounts that use daily systems
  • Restores that have never been tested
  • No agreed order for what to recover first after a disruption

What the service includes

Identification of critical business data

Working with you to identify the systems and data your business depends on.

Backup coverage and retention planning

Planning what is backed up, how often, and how long copies are kept.

Backup failure monitoring

Monitoring covered backup jobs and following up on failures.

Protected backup access

Restricting and strengthening access to backups to reduce the risk of tampering or deletion.

Recovery testing

Performing test restores to check that covered data can be recovered.

Agreed recovery priorities

Documenting which systems and data should be restored first.

Initial setup versus ongoing management

Phase 1

Initial setup

  • Critical data identification
  • Backup coverage and retention plan
  • Backup access protection
  • Initial recovery test
  • Recovery priorities documented

Phase 2

Ongoing management

  • Backup failure monitoring for covered backups
  • Periodic recovery tests at an agreed frequency
  • Coverage reviews as systems and data change
  • Updates to recovery priorities when the business changes

The exact setup activities, ongoing frequency, and covered systems are confirmed in your service plan.

What you receive

  • A summary of critical data and backup coverage
  • A documented retention plan
  • Recovery test results
  • A recovery priorities document
  • Backup status summaries if ongoing management is included

Scope and responsibilities

What we handle

  • Plan, configure, and monitor covered backups within the agreed scope
  • Run recovery tests and report results
  • Support restoration according to agreed priorities

What you handle

  • Help identify which data and systems are critical
  • Approve retention periods and storage costs
  • Tell us about new systems or data that should be covered
  • Make staff available to confirm restored data is correct

Recovery depends on the backups available, their condition, and the nature of the incident. Recovery tests show what can be restored under test conditions; they cannot guarantee every future recovery.

Frequently asked questions

Is cloud data already backed up by the provider?

Not always in the way businesses expect. We review what your providers retain and whether additional backups are needed.

How often do you test recovery?

Test frequency is agreed in your service plan based on how critical the data is.

Can you guarantee we get everything back?

No. We work to make recovery more reliable and to test it regularly, but outcomes depend on many factors.
