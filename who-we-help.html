Who We Help

Protection for the way your business works.

Every business depends on a different mix of accounts, devices, files, and online systems. We start with how your team actually works, then focus on the protections that matter most to you.

Professional services Construction & home services eCommerce & retail Agencies & distributed teams Growing businesses

Professional service businesses

Accounting, legal, consulting, financial advisory, and similar firms that handle client information and communicate largely by email.

Systems you depend on

  • Business email and calendars
  • Confidential client files and document storage
  • Practice management and accounting applications
  • Laptops used in the office and remotely

Common security concerns

  • Email impersonation and fraudulent requests to clients
  • Who can open confidential files, including former staff
  • Inconsistent access controls across applications
  • Employees unsure how to handle suspicious messages

Relevant services

  • Email & Account Security
  • Employee Security Training
  • Backup & Recovery
  • Endpoint & Device Security

Construction and home service companies

Contractors, trades, and field service businesses with crews on site, office staff handling payments, and work coordinated from phones and tablets.

Systems you depend on

  • Email used for quotes, invoices, and supplier payments
  • Shared files such as plans, bids, and job documents
  • Phones and tablets used in the field
  • Scheduling, estimating, and accounting applications

Common security concerns

  • Payment verification: requests to change bank details or redirect payments
  • Employee access when crews and subcontractors change frequently
  • Shared files with unclear sharing settings
  • Lost or unprotected mobile devices

Relevant services

  • Email & Account Security
  • Employee Security Training
  • Endpoint & Device Security
  • Backup & Recovery

eCommerce and retail businesses

Online stores and retailers whose website, store platform, and connected apps are central to taking orders and serving customers.

Systems you depend on

  • Store platforms such as Shopify, WooCommerce, or a custom site
  • Third-party apps, plugins, and integrations
  • Store administrator and staff accounts
  • Order, product, and customer data

Common security concerns

  • Store administrator access held by too many people or former contractors
  • Third-party apps with more access than they need
  • Website maintenance and updates applied inconsistently
  • How quickly the store could be recovered after a problem

Relevant services

  • Website & eCommerce Security
  • Backup & Recovery
  • Email & Account Security
  • Managed Security Monitoring

Agencies and distributed teams

Marketing, creative, and digital agencies, and other teams working across locations, often with freelancers and access to client accounts.

Systems you depend on

  • Cloud collaboration, file sharing, and messaging tools
  • Access to client websites, ad accounts, and platforms
  • Laptops used from home and while traveling
  • Many connected SaaS applications

Common security concerns

  • Shared logins and client access that outlast a project or contractor
  • Connected apps nobody remembers approving
  • Devices outside the office with inconsistent protection
  • Onboarding and offboarding across many tools

Relevant services

  • Email & Account Security
  • Endpoint & Device Security
  • Website & eCommerce Security
  • Employee Security Training

Growing businesses without an internal security team

Businesses that have added people, tools, and devices quickly, where security has been handled informally or alongside other jobs.

Systems you depend on

  • Business email and cloud accounts
  • A growing number of employee devices
  • Business data spread across several applications
  • A website and online tools customers rely on

Common security concerns

  • No clear picture of which systems, accounts, and devices exist
  • Nobody specifically responsible for security decisions
  • Uncertainty about whether backups would actually work
  • Not knowing where to start

Relevant services

  • Security Assessment
  • Managed Security Monitoring
  • Backup & Recovery
  • All services

Our services can support your wider security and governance efforts, but they do not by themselves make your business compliant with industry-specific regulations. If you have regulatory obligations, we will work alongside your legal, compliance, or industry advisors.
