How It Works

A clear path from understanding to ongoing protection.

We work in four phases so you always know what is happening, why, and who is responsible. Each phase builds on agreements made with you, not assumptions.

Four phases

  1. 1

    Assess

    Understand the business, inventory the agreed systems, and review existing protections.

    • Conversations about how your team works and what concerns you
    • An inventory of the systems, accounts, and devices agreed in scope
    • A review of protections and procedures already in place

    Outcome: a shared, documented picture of your current position.

  2. 2

    Prioritize

    Create an improvement plan based on business impact, urgency, and available resources.

    • Findings ranked by business impact and urgency
    • Recommendations sized to your budget, time, and staff
    • A sequence you agree with before any work begins

    Outcome: an improvement plan you have reviewed and approved.

  3. 3

    Protect

    Implement agreed controls, document responsibilities, and onboard employees.

    • Agreed controls put in place on covered systems
    • Responsibilities written down for us, you, and other providers
    • Employees introduced to changes that affect them

    Outcome: agreed protections in place and documented.

  4. 4

    Maintain

    Review alerts, updates, backups, training, and outstanding risks according to the service plan.

    • Ongoing activities at the frequency set in your service plan
    • Follow-up on outstanding risks from earlier phases
    • Adjustments as your team, tools, and priorities change

    Outcome: protections kept current rather than set once and forgotten.

Some businesses start with an assessment only. Ongoing maintenance is included only where it is part of your service plan.

Working with your existing IT provider

Many businesses already have an IT provider or an internal IT person. We are designed to work alongside them, not replace them. Clear responsibilities prevent gaps, duplicated work, and confusion during an incident.

Responsibilities agreed before service begins

Before any work starts, we agree in writing with you, and your IT provider where appropriate:

  • Which systems each party manages
  • Who makes, approves, and reviews changes
  • Who handles updates, backups, and user accounts
  • How administrative access is provided and recorded

Escalation contacts agreed before service begins

We also agree how issues are raised and handled:

  • Named contacts at your business and at your IT provider
  • Preferred contact methods and backup contacts
  • Which actions need your approval before they are taken
  • How we coordinate if an incident affects shared systems

If you do not have an IT provider, we will tell you which responsibilities would remain with your business and which can be covered by our services.
