Security Assessment

Understand where your business is exposed.

Start with a review of your business systems, access, and existing protections so you can prioritize the next steps.

Request an Assessment Book a Consultation

How the assessment works

Nothing is reviewed until we have spoken with you and agreed the scope together.

  1. 1

    Introductory conversation

    We talk with you about your business, the systems you rely on, and the concerns that prompted your request.

  2. 2

    Agreement on assessment scope

    We agree in writing which systems and information will be reviewed, how access will be provided, and what is out of scope.

  3. 3

    Review of agreed systems and information

    We review only the systems and information covered by the agreed scope, using the access you choose to provide.

  4. 4

    Findings and recommended next steps

    We walk you through what we found and the actions we recommend, in order of priority.

Potential review areas

The areas included depend on your business and the agreed scope. Common areas include:

  • Business email and user accounts
  • Employee devices
  • Website and hosting
  • Backups
  • Employee access
  • Third-party applications
  • Existing security procedures

What you receive

At the end of the assessment, you receive:

  • A summary of reviewed systems
  • Prioritized findings
  • Recommended corrective actions
  • A proposed implementation plan

Findings reflect the systems and information reviewed at the time of the assessment. Pricing and timing are discussed during the introductory conversation.

Sample deliverable

What a findings summary looks like

Each finding is written in plain language, ranked by priority, and paired with a recommended next step, so you can decide what to fix first.

  • Priority level for every finding
  • Why it matters to your business
  • A clear recommended action

What happens after you submit

  1. 1

    We review your request

    We read the details you shared to understand your environment and concerns.

  2. 2

    We contact you

    We reach out using your preferred contact method to arrange an introductory conversation.

  3. 3

    We agree on scope

    Together we confirm what will be reviewed, along with timing and pricing. Nothing is accessed without your written authorization.

  4. 4

    Assessment and report

    We carry out the agreed review and walk you through the prioritized findings.
