Services

Security services built around your business.

Each service focuses on one area your business depends on: accounts, devices, websites, data, people, and monitoring. Start with one area or combine several, based on what you need to protect.

Request a Security Assessment Book a Consultation

Service categories

Every service follows the same structure: an initial setup to establish a sound baseline, with optional ongoing management to keep it that way.

Email & Account Security

Protects: Business email, cloud accounts, and the people who sign in to them

This service strengthens how employees and administrators sign in, reviews who has access to what, and puts clear procedures in place for people joining and leaving your business.

Learn more →

Endpoint & Device Security

Protects: Laptops, desktops, and other devices employees use for work

This service establishes what devices you have, deploys endpoint protection, applies secure settings and encryption, and keeps operating systems and applications updated, with ongoing checks that protection stays healthy.

Learn more →

Website & eCommerce Security

Protects: Your website, online store, and the accounts and add-ons that run them

This service reviews how your site is accessed, configured, and maintained, and helps put monitoring, updates, and recovery plans in place.

Learn more →

Backup & Recovery

Protects: The business data you would need to keep operating after a disruption

This service identifies your critical data, plans backup coverage and retention, monitors for failures, and tests recovery against priorities agreed with you.

Learn more →

Employee Security Training

Protects: Your people, and the everyday decisions they make with email, links, and data

This service gives them short, practical training based on real situations, starting in their first week and refreshed throughout the year, with clear reporting instructions and completion reporting for managers.

Learn more →

Managed Security Monitoring

Protects: Covered systems where early warning of suspicious activity matters

This service monitors covered systems, reviews and escalates alerts, defines who does what during containment, and coordinates incident activity, with reporting for management.

Learn more →
