Services / Employee Security Training

Help your team recognize risks and respond confidently.

Employees handle email, payments, accounts, and devices every day. This service gives them short, practical training based on real situations, starting in their first week and refreshed throughout the year, with clear reporting instructions and completion reporting for managers.

Request a Security Assessment Book a Consultation

How the training works

Training is designed to fit into a normal workday and to build confidence, not fear.

Short, practical lessons

Brief lessons focused on one topic at a time, written in plain language and tied to tasks employees already do.

Real-world scenarios

Situations employees are likely to meet, such as unexpected sign-in prompts, payment change requests, and unfamiliar attachments.

New employee training

Security essentials covered during onboarding, so new team members know expectations from their first days.

Refresher quizzes

Short scenario-based quizzes that reinforce earlier lessons and highlight topics worth revisiting.

Clear incident reporting instructions

Simple, written steps telling employees who to contact and what to do when something looks wrong, and reassurance that reporting is always the right call.

Completion reporting for managers

Managers can see who has completed assigned training through a separately secured client training platform, not through this public website.

Business problems this service addresses

  • New employees start without any security guidance
  • One-time annual training that is quickly forgotten
  • Employees unsure what to do when something looks suspicious
  • No record of who has completed training

What the service includes

New employee onboarding

Security essentials for new employees as part of their first days.

Short recurring lessons

Brief lessons delivered on a regular schedule.

Scenario-based quizzes

Quizzes based on realistic workplace situations to reinforce lessons.

Approved phishing simulations

Phishing simulations run only with your written approval and agreed parameters.

Reporting instructions

Clear instructions telling employees how and where to report suspicious activity.

Completion tracking

Tracking which employees have completed assigned training, available to managers through a separately secured client training platform.

Initial setup versus ongoing management

Phase 1

Initial setup

  • Training plan and schedule agreed with you
  • Onboarding content prepared
  • Reporting instructions written for your business
  • Phishing simulation rules approved in writing, if included

Phase 2

Ongoing management

  • Recurring lessons and quizzes
  • Approved phishing simulations at an agreed frequency
  • Onboarding training for new employees
  • Completion tracking and follow-up

The exact setup activities, ongoing frequency, and covered systems are confirmed in your service plan.

What you receive

  • An agreed training plan
  • Written reporting instructions for employees
  • Completion reports
  • Phishing simulation summaries, if included

Scope and responsibilities

What we handle

  • Deliver training content according to the agreed plan
  • Run phishing simulations only as approved
  • Provide completion and simulation reporting

What you handle

  • Share employee lists and keep them current
  • Encourage participation and set expectations with staff
  • Approve simulation timing and content
  • Decide how training results are used internally

Training supports better decisions, but it does not guarantee employees will never make a mistake. It works best alongside technical controls.

Frequently asked questions

Will phishing simulations embarrass employees?

Simulations are intended for learning. We agree the approach with you and recommend a supportive, non-punitive follow-up.

How long are the lessons?

Lessons are designed to be short so they fit into a normal workday. Exact format is agreed in your plan.

Does this training satisfy compliance requirements?

Training records may support your compliance efforts, but completing training does not by itself make your business compliant.

Where are employee training records kept?

Employee records and manager reports are kept in a separately secured client training platform with access limited to authorized users. They are never part of this public website or the public demo.

Is the demo on this page the actual training?

No. The Security Awareness Demo is a short public example of the training style. Client training content and scheduling are agreed in your service plan.
