Email and phishing

Email is where many business security problems begin.

Quick guide

Spotting a suspicious message

  • Unexpected requests, especially urgent ones
  • Sender names that do not match the actual address
  • Links or attachments you were not expecting

Quick guide

Payment and invoice requests

  • Verify bank detail changes by phone, using a number on file
  • Do not rely on contact details in the request itself
  • Follow an agreed approval process for payments

Quick guide

What to do if you clicked

  • Tell your IT or security contact straight away
  • Describe what you clicked or entered
  • Do not delete the message until advised

Passwords and account access

Accounts are the keys to your business systems.

Quick guide

Multi-factor authentication basics

  • Turn on MFA for email and key business accounts
  • Prioritize administrator accounts first
  • Only approve sign-in requests you started

Quick guide

Better password habits

  • Use a different password for each account
  • Consider a business password manager
  • Never share passwords, even with colleagues

Quick guide

Keeping access tidy

  • Give people only the access their role needs
  • Limit the number of administrators
  • Remove access promptly when people leave

Website security

Your website and online store need regular care.

Quick guide

Administrator access

  • Give each administrator their own login
  • Remove former staff, agencies, and contractors
  • Turn on MFA where your platform supports it

Quick guide

Plugins, themes, and apps

  • Remove add-ons you no longer use
  • Keep the rest updated
  • Review what access each app has been given

Quick guide

Knowing who does what

  • Confirm what your platform or host handles
  • Agree who applies updates and when
  • Know who to call if the site goes down

Backups and recovery

A backup only helps if it can be restored.

Quick guide

What to back up

  • List the data you could not operate without
  • Include cloud applications, not just computers
  • Check what your providers already retain

Quick guide

Protecting your backups

  • Restrict who can delete or change backups
  • Keep at least one copy separate from daily systems
  • Check for backup failures regularly

Quick guide

Testing recovery

  • Restore a sample of files periodically
  • Agree which systems to recover first
  • Write down the steps while they are fresh

Employee security habits

Small daily habits make a meaningful difference.

Quick guide

Everyday device habits

  • Lock your screen when you step away
  • Install updates when prompted
  • Report lost or stolen devices immediately

Quick guide

Building a reporting culture

  • Make it clear who to contact and how
  • Thank people for reporting, even false alarms
  • Never punish honest mistakes

Business owner checklists

Questions worth asking about your own business.

Checklist

Accounts and access

  • Is MFA on for email and administrator accounts?
  • Do we know who has administrator access?
  • Is access removed when someone leaves?

Checklist

Devices and data

  • Do we have a list of work devices?
  • Are laptops encrypted and kept updated?
  • Have we tested restoring from backup?

Checklist

People and response

  • Do employees know how to report concerns?
  • Do we verify payment change requests?
  • Do we know who to call if something goes wrong?
