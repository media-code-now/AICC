Services / Managed Security Monitoring

Get suspicious activity reviewed and escalated with clear next steps.

Security tools generate alerts, but someone needs to review them and decide what happens next. This service monitors covered systems, reviews and escalates alerts, defines who does what during containment, and coordinates incident activity, with reporting for management.

Request a Security Assessment Book a Consultation

Business problems this service addresses

  • Alerts from security tools that nobody reviews consistently
  • Uncertainty about who should act when something suspicious happens
  • No agreed process for containing an issue or coordinating a response
  • Leadership without a clear view of security activity

What the service includes

Monitoring for covered systems

Monitoring the systems and alert sources defined in your service plan.

Alert review and escalation

Reviewing alerts and escalating those that need action to your nominated contacts.

Defined containment responsibilities

Agreeing in advance which containment actions we may take and which require your approval.

Incident coordination

Coordinating response activity between your team, your providers, and us during an incident.

Management reporting

Regular reports summarizing monitoring activity and notable events.

Initial setup versus ongoing management

Phase 1

Initial setup

  • Covered systems and alert sources defined
  • Escalation contacts and channels agreed
  • Containment responsibilities documented and approved
  • Reporting format and frequency agreed

Phase 2

Ongoing management

  • Monitoring and alert review for covered systems
  • Escalation according to the agreed process
  • Incident coordination when needed
  • Management reporting

The exact setup activities, ongoing frequency, and covered systems are confirmed in your service plan.

What you receive

  • A documented monitoring scope
  • An escalation and containment responsibilities document
  • Regular management reports
  • Incident summaries when incidents occur

Scope and responsibilities

What we handle

  • Monitor covered systems and review alerts
  • Escalate to your contacts according to the agreed process
  • Take only the containment actions you have approved in advance

What you handle

  • Keep escalation contacts current and reachable
  • Make decisions on actions that require your approval
  • Maintain the tools and licenses that generate alerts, unless included in the plan
  • Notify us of changes to covered systems

Monitoring coverage is defined in your service plan. Systems and alert sources outside that scope are not monitored.

Frequently asked questions

What hours is monitoring provided?

Monitoring coverage is defined in your service plan.

Will you shut down systems without asking?

No. Containment responsibilities are agreed in advance, and actions outside that agreement require your approval.

Does monitoring detect every threat?

No. Monitoring improves visibility into covered systems, but it cannot detect every threat or prevent every incident.
